Small pieces.

BlueMath is an open-source Python framework for the statistical analysis and simulation of coastal climate hazards. We broke years of research into small, reusable pieces, and anyone can snap them together into methods and climate services.

Why we built it

Most coastal research is hard to reuse.

70%

of researchers failed to reproduce someone else's published research.

Baker, 2016
>50%

failed to reproduce their own experiments.

Baker, 2016
11%

of the analyzed papers provided their code.

Jansen et al., 2020

Research slows down when every new method has to be rebuilt from scratch before it fits into an existing workflow. BlueMath turns that work into shared pieces that are written once, tested, and reused.

How it came together

From papers to pieces to a shared repository.

  1. Identify the pieces

    We went back through a decade of GeoOcean projects and publications and found the same building blocks again and again: clustering, case selection, interpolation, model runs.

  2. Organize them

    Each piece was cleaned up and sorted by what it does: data handling, statistics and machine learning, numerical model wrappers, and coastal domain tools.

  3. Open the repository

    The pieces live together in one open-source Python repository, with the methods and services built from them.
